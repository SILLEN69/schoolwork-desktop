An assistant for the work in front of you.

SchoolWork helps you code, research and follow lessons using a school-provided TeachGPT model. Projects, conversations and lesson notes are stored on your computer. An eligible TeachGPT account and your own API key are required.

Windows downloads and release notes

Connect your Google account

The upcoming Google-enabled build opens your usual browser when you select Sign in with Google. Choose your account and grant Calendar event permission. Optional Drive read access is off by default. You do not create your own Google Cloud project.

Availability: the shared Google integration is being prepared for verification. It is currently limited to configured test accounts. A download does not imply Google has approved the app. Existing v0.10.0 installations use a custom Google client.

Calendar and Drive, when you ask

Review assignment titles and dates before saving an event to Calendar. Ask the assistant to read events for a date range or, if you enabled Drive, find a document and read its text. Requested results may be sent to your selected TeachGPT endpoint to answer your question. Read the privacy policy before connecting.
