# SchoolWork launch ad — Less busywork. More done.

Production script, landscape only. **1920 × 1080, 30 fps, 32 seconds / 960 frames.** Eight scenes. An upbeat benefit-led advertisement using animated UI inspired by the current SchoolWork app. This document proposes a new edit; it does not modify or render the previous video.

## Creative direction

Sell the feeling of turning a busy school day into useful outcomes. Start with a benefit, introduce SchoolWork, then demonstrate a single English lesson becoming notes, a calendar assignment, an email and a usable revision site.

Keep SchoolWork's existing linked-square mark, dark green surfaces and lime accent: background #111614, surfaces #191f1c, borders #303a33, accent #D3EC95, text #EDF0E8. UI typography follows the app; ad headlines use a strong, clean sans serif with tight spacing. Headline size approximately 150–190 px, supporting copy 46–58 px. Use Windows-style app chrome. Do not repeat a fixed headline-left/window-right layout throughout.

Camera movement follows the action: pull out to establish the app, push into an important control, pan to the result. Gentle perspective changes and deliberate match cuts give the interface depth. An assignment card can leave the app and become a Calendar event; a message bubble can expand into an email draft. Full-screen type provides punctuation between product shots.

Every transition should lead somewhere useful. Give major text and success states a readable hold. Freeze the camera briefly around important clicks. No idle particles, random shaking, constant zooming or perpetual pulsing. Animated screens were requested; do not add a demo-mode label or imply these shots are a live performance benchmark.

## Pointer art direction

Use a cleaner computer-use cursor inspired by the clarity of OpenAI's product treatment, in SchoolWork green. This is an inspired design, not a claim to reproduce OpenAI's exact cursor asset.

- A rounded classic arrow silhouette, 46 × 56 px at the composition's UI scale, with a pale-lime to green fill, a fine dark-green outline and a restrained shadow.
- Remove the tiny linked-square logo inside the cursor. Keep the SchoolWork logo in the app chrome and closing card instead.
- Add a faint green halo close to the arrow; no large badge or opaque dark rectangle.
- Movement uses smooth curved paths with brief deceleration near the target. The arrow tip follows the actual click point.
- On click, compress to 0.94 scale for three frames, return to 1 by frame eight, and let a thin green ripple expand for 12–16 frames.
- Keep the pointer visible through the confirmation state. Do not cut it away before the click ripple finishes.
- The small speech bubble is translucent and resizes around a short public explanation. Offset it far enough from the arrow to keep Send unobstructed. No tool names, raw reasoning or permanently flashing labels.

## Music and sound direction

Use a more energetic **150 BPM electronic/garage-inspired track**, ideally composed for the edit or licensed for this use. Punchy kick, tight clap/snare, syncopated bass and bright rhythmic synth stabs. No sung lyrics competing with captions. The reference track is a mood reference, not an asset already cleared for SchoolWork.

At 150 BPM, one beat is **0.4 seconds / 12 frames**. All scene boundaries land on beats. Total: 80 beats / 20 bars.

- Start with a beat and a short reverse intake; do not hide the hook behind a long fade-in.
- Add percussion at the app reveal, open the full groove for lesson capture and notes, and use a fill into the Calendar scene.
- Briefly remove the bass for the email request; bring it back with the computer-use reveal.
- The Gmail Send click gets a click transient plus a clean tonal confirmation. The music makes space for it.
- Build energy during Work mode, then resolve onto the end card with a held brand chord. Fade the final tail during the last 0.8 seconds.
- Text entrances use dry, restrained ticks or soft impacts; larger transitions use short directional swishes. Sounds follow the visual event, not every word.
- Mix for social playback around -14 to -16 LUFS with controlled peaks. Music remains the dominant bed; clicks sit clearly above it without becoming loud typing noise.
