Privacy policy

Effective 11 October 2026. This policy describes the SchoolWork Windows application maintained by Elias Sillén (SILLEN69). Contact elias.sillen@gmail.com with privacy questions. SchoolWork is independent of Google and Stockholm Science and Innovation School.

Data on your computer

SchoolWork stores settings, conversations, tool results, project knowledge, lesson audio and transcripts in its local application profile and workspace. Files and Markdown memory notes are ordinary local files. OAuth refresh tokens and the TeachGPT key are protected locally using Electron safeStorage on Windows. This protection does not encrypt all conversation or workspace contents.

Optional Google connection

Google sign-in happens in your normal browser using a desktop OAuth client, PKCE and a temporary local callback. SchoolWork does not receive your Google password. Each user authorizes their own account; the publisher's shared client is not a user access token.

Calendar event access allows SchoolWork to read events for a requested date range and to create assignment events you review and confirm. The permission Google grants is broader than the set of actions currently exposed by the application. Calendar event details include titles, descriptions, dates, identifiers and links. Event identifiers and links may be saved in local lesson records.

Drive access is optional, off by default and read-only. When you enable it and approve Google permission, SchoolWork can search file names, retrieve file metadata and read requested Google Docs, text, Markdown and CSV documents. The Google permission permits seeing and downloading all Drive files; the app currently retrieves documents in response to your requests. It does not provide Drive write or sharing tools.

Use and transfer to AI services

SchoolWork uses Google data only for the user-facing features you request: planning your assignments, answering questions about events and understanding selected documents. When you connect, the app discloses that requested Calendar details and Drive excerpts may be included in the context sent directly from your computer to your selected TeachGPT endpoint. Google access tokens are never included in model context. Keep Google disconnected if you do not want this integration.

The TeachGPT service is operated separately from SchoolWork. Its operator processes requests under its own applicable terms and account rules. Check those terms before sending private information. The SchoolWork publisher does not receive your conversations through an application backend. SchoolWork does not train models, sell Google data, serve targeted ads from it or transfer it to advertising services. Any service used to process Google-derived data must also comply with Google's Limited Use requirements; a compliant service configuration is a prerequisite to production availability.

Retention and your controls

Local history remains until you remove it. Disconnect Google in Connected apps to remove the local refresh token; SchoolWork also attempts to revoke the Google grant. You can independently revoke access in your Google account's third-party connections settings. Disconnecting does not delete existing Calendar events, prior conversation content, exported files or lesson transcripts. Remove those separately in SchoolWork, your filesystem or Google Calendar. Forgetting a memory note does not delete its original conversation.

The publisher cannot remotely inspect or delete your local profile. For support, share only the specific redacted diagnostic information you intend to provide. Review exported files and screenshots before sharing them.

Other network services

TeachGPT requests require your configured account key. Optional features such as Telegram, web research and explicitly configured MCP connections contact the services you enable. The application checks GitHub for updates and downloads releases when requested. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process ordinary web request information under its own privacy policy. The publisher has not added website tracking scripts or analytics.

Google Limited Use

SchoolWork's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used for the disclosed user-facing features, not general-purpose model training. Google verification remains pending; this policy is not a claim of approval.

Changes

Updates will be published here. Material changes to Google-data use require notice and renewed consent before that new use begins.
